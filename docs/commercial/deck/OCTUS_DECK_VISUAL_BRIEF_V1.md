## Brand

- Use canonical Octus brand assets from `docs/design/BRAND_MASTER_ASSETS/` and `public/brand/`  
- Do not invent a new visual system for the deck  

## Tone

- Institutional, compact, calm  
- Presence first; conversion second  
- Concrete labels over metaphors  

## Layout

- One idea per slide  
- Prefer maps and short lists over paragraphs  
- Max ~6 bullets where lists appear  
- Disclaimers as quiet footer, not banners  

## Avoid

- Purple-glow AI aesthetics  
- Dense dashboard slides  
- Price tables  
- Partner logos  
- Stock “handshake” hero clichés
