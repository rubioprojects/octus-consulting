## Required sections (every playbook)

1. **simple_explanation** — plain language, one short paragraph  
2. **client_profile** — who buys  
3. **triggers** — why they call now  
4. **scope** — what Octus does  
5. **deliverables** — concrete outputs  
6. **exclusions** — what is not included  
7. **discovery_questions** — what Milla asks  
8. **qualification_criteria** — when to pursue  
9. **red_flags** — when to pause or escalate  
10. **dependencies** — other areas needed  
11. **related_services** — SVC-* links  
12. **cross_sell** — natural next offers  
13. **proposal_model** — how to package  
14. **allowed_claims** — CL-* only  
15. **sources** — evidence paths  
16. **owner** — commercial owner  
17. **review_date** — next review
