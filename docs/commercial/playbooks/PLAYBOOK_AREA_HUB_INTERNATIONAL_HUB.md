## simple_explanation

Octus finds, connects and coordinates the banks, PSPs, CSPs, labs, counsel and other specialists a regulated programme needs. Partners are not named publicly. Introductions are not guaranteed outcomes.

## client_profile

Operators who know what they need to build and need the right providers sequenced under one Octus relationship.

## triggers

- Needs bank / PSP options after readiness work
- Needs CSP, local director or formation partners
- Needs lab / certification path
- Needs counsel or tax specialists in jurisdiction
- Multi-provider market entry

## scope

- Market entry coordination
- Bank and PSP introductions
- Payment and PSP architecture
- CSP, local director and formation partners
- Lab, certification and specialist network
- Counsel and tax specialist coordination

## deliverables

- Provider shortlist (unnamed in public materials)
- Introduction sequence
- Coordination plan
- Role map of who does what
- Handoff into Regulatory / Corporate / Compliance workstreams

## exclusions

- Marketplace listing of named partners
- Promised introduction SLAs
- Promised bank / PSP / certification outcomes
- Replacing Regulatory, Corporate or Remediation work

## discovery_questions

1. Which provider types are missing?
2. Which jurisdictions?
3. What has already failed or been refused?
4. Timing pressure?
5. Who must approve introductions on the client side?
