## simple_explanation

Octus designs companies, holdings and cross-border structures for regulated operations — including banking readiness and corporate accounting/tax coordination. Company formation sits inside that design; it is not a formation shop.

## client_profile

Grupos multi-país; founders que precisam separar risco; UBOs atrás de operações reguladas.

## triggers

- Abrir empresa para licença/banco
- Holding / reorganização
- UBO disclosure pressure
- Estrutura atual bloqueia banking ou licence

## scope

- Entity and holding architecture
- Company formation in-architecture
- Cross-border structuring
- Corporate reorganisation
- Banking readiness (remapped from former AREA-BNK)
- Accounting and finance coordination (not an eighth Tax area)
- Corporate tax planning (Octus-sold; partners unnamed)

## deliverables

- Structure memo
- Formation plan
- Reorg plan
- Banking readiness pack
- Accounting / tax coordination plan

## exclusions

- “Cheap offshore” tone
- Aggressive tax planning without counsel
- Promised tax benefits or bank accounts
- Public OC8 / partner brand names
- Personal UBO/private-client work as primary (see AREA-PC)

## discovery_questions

1. Quem são os UBOs?
2. Onde as operações e clientes estão?
3. Qual licença/banking depende desta estrutura?
4. Há deadlines de incorporação?
5. Já existem entities? Quais?

## qualification_criteria

- UBOs dispostos a KYC
- Objetivo operacional claro (licence/bank)
- Não é pedido puramente de anonimato

## red_flags

- Pedido de ocultar UBO
- Formação sem pathway regulatório
- Conflito entre narrativa e documentos
