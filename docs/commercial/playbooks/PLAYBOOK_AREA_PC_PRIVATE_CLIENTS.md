## simple_explanation

Support for founders, UBOs, executives and families tied to regulated operations — personal ownership, holdings, residency, personal banking readiness and personal tax coordination.

## client_profile

Individuals behind regulated companies who need personal structure to move with the business without mixing personal and company risk.

## triggers

- Founder / UBO structure unclear
- Personal holdings need reorganisation
- Residency or mobility linked to ops
- Personal banking / KYC blocked
- Personal tax needs coordination with corporate plan

## scope

- Ownership and UBO architecture
- Personal holding and asset organisation
- Residency and personal international structuring
- Personal banking and individual KYC readiness
- Personal tax coordination

## deliverables

- Personal structure map
- Holding options
- Residency pathway notes
- Personal banking readiness pack
- Personal tax coordination plan (specialists unnamed)

## exclusions

- Treating Private Clients as marketing segment only
- Public partner brand names
- Promised residency or bank outcomes
- Eighth Tax pillar branding

## discovery_questions

1. Who are the individuals behind the operating structure?
2. What is personal vs company?
3. Residency / mobility constraints?
4. Personal banking status?
5. Personal tax jurisdictions?
