## Where claims may exceed evidence (review before amplify)

- Timeline / outcome specifics in mandates  
- Certification language vs operational proof preference  
- Jurisdiction pages as if continuously maintained legal advice  

## Fragmentation vs peers

Peers usually show **few service lines** with depth. Octus shows **many parallel objects** (7 modules + 12 solutions + jurisdictions). Differentiation exists in copy but is diluted by catalogue shape.

## Differentiation not yet visible enough

1. Lead contractor accountability (About has it; nav/footer don’t)  
2. Multi-layer simultaneous execution (regulatory + banking + corporate)  
3. Intelligence → commercial loop (Insights teaser only)

## Recommended quality moves (no copy theft)

1. Collapse to fewer Solution lines publicly.  
2. Make engagement models first-class.  
3. Rebalance Insights toward markets claimed on Markets pages.  
4. Frame jurisdictions as **constraints**, not SKUs.  
5. Add a restrained intelligence narrative without shipping confidential Axle detail.
