# Octus — Product / Service Taxonomy Recommendation

**Baseline:** `602ae189` · Hypothesis evaluated against current IA · **Not auto-implemented**

## Problem with current hierarchy

Everything is presented at similar weight:

- Module pillars ≈ Solutions deep pages ≈ Markets ≈ Jurisdictions ≈ “What we do”

Buyers cannot tell **what to buy** vs **where Octus operates** vs **how engagement works**.

## Distinctions (definitions)

| Term | Meaning on Octus site | Example |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| **Solution** | Packaged response to a structural problem | Banking & Payments remediation |
| **Market** | Industry context that shapes the solution | iGaming, Fintech |
| **Jurisdiction** | Venue constraints / options — **not** the product | Malta, Brazil |
| **Capability** | Discipline Octus can deploy inside a solution | Legal, Compliance, Banking |
| **Operating module** | Internal org of work (current “7 modules”) | Should not be primary nav |
| **Engagement model** | Commercial relationship shape | Lead contractor / managed project |

## Recommended commercial architecture (preferred)

### 1. SOLUTIONS (primary commercial taxonomy)

| Solution | One-sentence proposition | Maps from today |
|----------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| Regulatory and Licensing | Design and execute the licensing path so structure passes before filing | `/regulatory`, igaming/fintech licensing, market entry |
| Banking and Payments | Make the operation bankable and payment-resilient | banking-payments + high-risk banking narrative |
| Compliance and Risk | Operate compliance as a system under audit and banking pressure | compliance solutions + `/compliance` |
| Corporate and Cross-Border Structuring | Design entities and holdings for license, bank, and expansion | corporate solutions + `/corporate` |
| Market Entry and Expansion | Coordinate jurisdiction, corporate, license, and banking as one project | market-entry + international-hub (refined) |
| Complex Operational Remediation | Unstick blocked licenses, banking, and post-license failure | homepage mandates + diagnostic |

**Legal** stays a **capability inside** solutions (contracts, analysis), not a separate shopfront labelled like a law firm — unless human confirms a distinct Legal Solutions page.

### 2. MARKETS

- iGaming and Betting — KEEP  
- Fintech and Payments — KEEP  
- Digital Assets — KEEP (rename Crypto → Digital Assets in nav copy when approved)  
- Other Highly Regulated Markets — CREATE as umbrella (absorb “high-risk” framing carefully)

### 3. OPERATING CAPABILITIES (About / How we work — not primary nav)

Legal · Regulatory · Compliance · Corporate · Banking · Technology · Intelligence  

Use on About and solution pages as “how we staff the work,” not as SKUs.

### 4. ENGAGEMENT MODELS (homepage + contact)

| Model | Current site | Recommendation |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Lead Contractor | Strong on About; weak as purchasable model | **CREATE** explicit section |
| Managed Project | Homepage “Project” | KEEP / rename |

Homepage today: Project / Ongoing / Hour Package — **incomplete** vs lead-contractor story. Prefer the five models above after human approval.

## Candidate structure vs current site

| Candidate | Fit | Notes |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Mandate’s 6 Solutions | Strong | Aligns with deep pages; collapses 7 modules |
| Current 7 modules as primary | Weak | Feels internal; duplicates Solutions |

## Final model recommendation

**Adopt Solutions (6) + Markets (4) + Engagement models (5) + Jurisdictions as supporting education.**  
Deprecate `/what-we-do` as a primary commercial page (redirect or merge into `/solutions` after approval).  
Keep pillar URLs temporarily for SEO; canonical narrative moves to Solutions.

**Human approval required** before URL collapses or nav changes.
