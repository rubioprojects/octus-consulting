## On-page SEO rules (post-launch)

1. One H1 per page (already mostly true).
2. Titles: unique, single `| Octus Consulting` brand suffix.
3. Meta descriptions: unique, ~140–160 chars, no unsupported claims (`premium`, `leading`, `guaranteed`, invented numbers).
4. Canonical + OG/Twitter always production origin (`https://octusconsulting.com`).
5. Insights: only public articles in sitemap; HOLD never indexable.
6. Internal links: Services ↔ Industries ↔ Jurisdictions ↔ Insights already provide cluster coverage — extend carefully, do not redesign.
