## Separation rules

1. Partners must not be mixed with employees on `/team`.
2. Internal operations (people ops, pure coordination) are not automatically public.
3. Catalog substantive ownership ≠ automatic public Team listing (Irati is the canonical example).
4. Team communicates accountability, not full payroll.
5. Area owners must be visually identifiable when published; specialists only when both commercially and institutionally relevant.
