LEAD INTELLIGENCE (built-in, zero config)
─────────────────────────────────
The diagnostic form includes automatic lead scoring.
Emails arrive with:
- Subject: [HIGH] / [MEDIUM] / [LOW] + company name
- Lead Intelligence block: priority, score, detected signals
- Timestamp UTC
Scoring is heuristic based on keywords (sector, licensing,
compliance, jurisdictions, banking, expansion, etc.).
No CRM needed. Triagem visual direto no inbox.

RESEND SETUP
─────────────────────────────────
1. Go to resend.com → create free account
2. Add and verify domain: octusconsulting.com (or subdomain send.octusconsulting.com)
3. Copy API key
4. In Vercel: Settings → Environment Variables → add RESEND_API_KEY
5. Redeploy
